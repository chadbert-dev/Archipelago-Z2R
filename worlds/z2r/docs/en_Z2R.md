# Z2R

## Where is the options page?

Z2R seeds are generated by the Z2R app, not from a web options page. The app writes the `locations`, `rules`,
`starting_items` and container options that go into your player YAML. See the setup guide.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Z2R shuffles items, palaces, towns and the overworld. In Archipelago mode every major item location (items, spells,
sword techniques, heart and magic containers and quest items) holds an Archipelago placeholder that gives nothing
when picked up. The multiworld decides what each of those locations gives, and items for you arrive from the
multiworld and are added to your inventory by the client. P-bags, jars, small keys and 1-ups are not shuffled.

## What is the goal?

Defeat Dark Link in the Great Palace.

## What items and locations get shuffled?

Every location that holds an Archipelago placeholder in your ROM, and the matching major items.

## What happens when the player receives an item?

The BizHawk client sets the matching inventory flag or container count in the game's memory.

## Unique local commands

None. Use `/bh` and `/toggle_text` from the BizHawk client.
